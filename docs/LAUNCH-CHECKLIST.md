# AI SaaS Launch Checklist

**Created by School of AI**

- Product: target user, activation event, success metric and support owner are clear.
- Tenancy: isolation verified in API, database, cache, jobs, retrieval and exports.
- Identity: authentication, recovery and privileged support access reviewed.
- Entitlements: plan limits and usage metering are enforced server-side.
- AI: structured outputs, evaluation, cost limits, fallbacks and sensitive-data rules exist.
- Agents: tools are least-privilege, consequential actions require approval where appropriate, and actions are audited.
- Reliability: retries are bounded and side effects are idempotent.
- Security: validation, secrets, dependencies and abuse controls reviewed.
- Testing: critical journeys and cross-tenant negative tests pass.
- Operations: logs, metrics, health, alerts, runbooks and support path exist.
- Delivery: migrations, deployment and rollback are rehearsed.
